# Prospect Scout rubric (first test, starter: tune after the first run)

1. `prospects.md` and `prospects.csv` both exist in /mnt/session/outputs/ and list the same companies.
2. The report contains between 0 and 3 companies, and each company has all five sections: why it fits, evidence of operational need, service match, likely buyer, what we still need to verify.
3. Each company's headcount (50 to 200) is backed by a cited source, or is explicitly marked unverified.
4. Each company has at least one operational signal with a working source URL and a date within the 12 months before the run date.
5. Every claim in the evidence section is labeled Verified (with URL and date) or Hypothesis (with stated reasoning).
6. No company is justified by growth alone: each has a specific signal tied to operational complexity.
7. No personal contact information appears (emails, phone numbers). Any named person is backed by a dated public source showing their role.
8. Each service match references that company's specific evidence rather than a generic pitch.
9. Each "what we still need to verify" list has at least two concrete items.
10. If fewer than 3 companies are returned, the report explains why (for example, candidates rejected for weak evidence).
